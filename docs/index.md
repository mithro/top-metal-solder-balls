# Solder balls on wafer.space top metal

Research into packaging methods that apply material directly to the aluminium
top metal of [wafer.space](https://wafer.space) GF180MCU chips, so that a bare
die can carry solder balls and be reflowed onto a board like a wafer-level chip
scale package.

This is a literature and vendor survey. Nothing here has been tried on real
die yet.

```{figure} /_static/diagrams/enig-cross-section.svg
:alt: Cross-section of a solder ball on an aluminium pad with a nickel gold under bump metal
:width: 100%

The leading candidate: electroless nickel and immersion gold on the pad, then a
250 um solder ball.
```
