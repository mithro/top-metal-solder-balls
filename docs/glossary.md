# Glossary

The terms used on this site, each with a short description and a link to the
page that explains it.

```{glossary}
ACF
  Anisotropic conductive film. An adhesive film loaded with conductive
  particles that connects pads through its thickness and insulates sideways.
  It is bonded with heat and pressure. See
  [Flex PCB and Z-axis tape](methods/flex-ztape.md).

Bump on pad
  A solder ball that sits directly over the bond pad, with no redistribution
  layer moving it elsewhere. See [Starting point](background.md).

Chip-on-board
  A die glued to a small circuit board and wire bonded to it. wafer.space
  sells this as a delivery option. See
  [Past wafer.space runs](past-runs.md).

ENIG
  Electroless nickel, immersion gold. A wet plating sequence that puts
  nickel and a thin gold cap on exposed metal with no mask. See the
  [ENIG lab recipe](industry/enig.md).

FC01
  A flip chip pad test die on wafer.space Run 2, with six large aluminium
  pads. See [Past wafer.space runs](past-runs.md).

Flip chip
  A die mounted face down, joined to the board by bumps or balls on its pads.

Intermetallic
  A compound that forms where two metals meet, such as gold and aluminium or
  tin and nickel. Some are brittle. See
  [Stud bumping](industry/stud-bump.md).

kÅ
  Kiloångström, the unit foundries use for film thickness. 1 kÅ is 0.1 µm, so
  the 11 kÅ top metal is 1.1 µm thick.

Passivation
  The insulating layer over the top of the die. Openings in it expose the
  pads. See [Starting point](background.md).

RDL
  Redistribution layer. Copper traces added on top of a finished die to move
  connections from the pads to ball positions. See
  [Wafer-level bumping](industry/wafer-level.md).

Reballing
  Placing solder balls on pads with a stencil and melting them in place. See
  [Reballing](methods/reballing.md).

SAC
  Tin-silver-copper solder, the common lead-free alloy.

Stud bump
  A gold or copper bump left on a pad by a wire bonder that bonds a ball and
  breaks the wire off. See [Stud bumping](industry/stud-bump.md).

Top metal
  The uppermost metal layer of the chip, which forms the pads. On wafer.space
  GF180MCU die it is 1.1 µm of aluminium. See
  [Starting point](background.md).

UBM
  Under-bump metal. The layers between the aluminium pad and the solder that
  solder can wet and that keep tin away from the aluminium. See
  [Starting point](background.md).

WLCSP
  Wafer-level chip scale package. A die with solder balls on its face,
  soldered straight to a board. See [Starting point](background.md).

Z-axis tape
  A pressure-sensitive adhesive tape that conducts through its thickness
  only. It is coarser than ACF. See
  [Flex PCB and Z-axis tape](methods/flex-ztape.md).

Zincate
  An alkaline zinc solution. Dipping aluminium in it replaces the surface
  oxide with a thin zinc layer that nickel can plate on. See
  [Zincate and nickel kit](methods/zincate-nickel.md).
```
