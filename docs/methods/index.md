# Home methods

One page per home method. Each page covers how the method works, what to buy,
rough cost, what is known and what is guesswork, and how well it fits loose
wafer.space die. The pages are in the order of the
[ranking](../summary.md), with [reballing](reballing.md), which several
methods share, last.

| Method | What it does | Fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Zincate and nickel kit](zincate-nickel.md) | Plates nickel on the pad | high |
| [Aluminium solder paste and flux](aluminium-solder.md) | Solders straight to the pad | high as a test |
| [Flex PCB and Z-axis tape](flex-ztape.md) | Glues the die to a flex that carries the balls | medium to high |
| [Silver epoxy dots](adhesives.md) | Glues each pad to the board | medium |
| [Silver deposits](silver.md) | Coats the pad with silver | low for solder, medium under epoxy |
| [Reballing](reballing.md) | Puts balls on pads that solder wets | high |

[Comparison](../comparison.md) adds steps, cost, evidence and whether each method gives solder balls.
